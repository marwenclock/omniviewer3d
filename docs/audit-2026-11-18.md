# OMNIVIEWER: independent audit and refresh, 18 November 2026

This adds to the reconciled audit package from 17 November (D01–D10) and my review of it
(`docs/review-2026-11-18-fresh-delta.md`). It covers design and identity, ease of use, quality-of-life details,
format coverage, the converter, growth and SEO, and new features. **Section 1 lists what I already built on the
`refresh-2026-11` branch.** Section 2 onwards is the audit itself, with what remains, ranked.

Method: I read the code, ran the build and the test suite (22 of 22 pass), and used the built site in a desktop
browser (Chromium) at 1440 px and 375 px, in light and dark themes. Nothing was deployed. Not checked: real phones,
Safari, screen readers, real customer files in the new formats, or native-language review.

## 2. About "every single 3D format"

Being straight about this matters, because the site's credibility depends on it.

- **Open formats** (geometry stored in a documented way) can all be supported in the browser. With this branch
  OMNIVIEWER covers the large majority of those people actually exchange. The remaining realistic ones:

  | Format | Feasibility | Cost |
  |---|---|---|
  | LAZ (compressed LiDAR) | `laz-perf` WASM, lazy-loaded | M |
  | LDraw `.ldr/.mpd` (LEGO) | three.js loader exists, but needs the parts library (about 40 MB) on demand: an online dependency to disclose | M |
  | E57 scans | no maintained JS reader; would need a WASM port of libE57 | L |
  | FBX **export** | no exporter in three.js; an ASCII FBX 7.4 writer is feasible (meshes, materials, hierarchy) and highly requested for Unity and Unreal | M–L |
  | Faceted **STEP export** ("STL to STEP") | a STEP writer that emits each triangle as a face is feasible, and "stl to step" is a very common search. It doesn't make a real CAD solid; say so | M |
  | DWG | `libredwg` WASM exists but is GPL (this repo is MIT): a licensing decision | L |
  | `.x`, `.ase`, `.ms3d`, `.smd` (legacy game formats) | small parsers each, low demand | S each |
  | 3MF/glTF compression on export (Draco, Meshopt, KTX2) | WASM encoders, "Compress for web" toggle | M |

- **Closed CAD and app formats** (Fusion 360 `.f3d`, SolidWorks, Inventor, CATIA, NX/Creo, SketchUp `.skp`,
  Blender `.blend`, 3ds Max, Maya, Cinema 4D, Revit) **cannot be opened by any browser tool without the vendor.**
  Their geometry is stored in closed kernels (Autodesk ShapeManager, Parasolid, ACIS) or in version-specific memory
  dumps. Websites that "open F3D" upload the file to Autodesk's cloud (Platform Services) to translate it. Doing
  that would break OMNIVIEWER's "never uploaded" promise and would need an Autodesk account and API keys. **My
  recommendation is to not do it.** The branch handles these files as well as a local tool honestly can: it
  recognises them, shows the preview stored inside, gives the exact export steps, and has guide pages that capture
  the "how do I open an .f3d file" search traffic. If you ever want an opt-in "Send to Autodesk for conversion"
  button, it must be clearly labelled as an upload and stay off by default.

## 3. Design and identity

**What was wrong.** The product was visually Bankasi Media's site with a 3D canvas in it: the same near-black
monochrome, the same card style and wide-tracked labels, and nothing that belonged to OMNIVIEWER. The workspace is
good, but nothing made it memorable.

**Direction (started on this branch):** *a precise instrument, strictly monochrome.*

1. **One signal, no hue.** The accent is the strongest contrast in each theme (white on dark, black on light). It
   marks only what is actionable or active: the primary action, the selected format, "recommended" and the active
   workspace. Status keeps its own colours (warn amber, danger red, OK green) and the axes keep red/green/blue.
2. **Calm surfaces.** Solid backgrounds with a soft radial gradient on the welcome screen; no patterns or depth
   effects behind content.
3. **Wordmark.** Keep the eye mark. Suggested next step: move "by BANKASI MEDIA" from the header to the footer and
   About on working screens, keeping it on the welcome screen only. That gives OMNIVIEWER its own name in the
   header, which is your stated goal.
4. **Personality through behaviour, not decoration.** What will make it feel new is what it does: the searchable
   converter, the task chips, recognising mislabelled files, the honest closed-format helper. Keep visual
   additions minimal.
5. **Type.** Display type stays on welcome and landing pages. Instrument numbers stay mono with units. Avoid
   uppercase labels in the working UI (they are a Bankasi signature): keep them only for small section headers.

**Remaining design items, ranked:**

| # | Item | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Give the share images a distinct OMNIVIEWER layout (they are generated from one SVG in `ogSvg`) | 595 social cards look like Bankasi pages |
| 2 | The left half of the welcome screen is dense on 1440 px: drop zone, 8 chips, trust line and recents. Move recents to a compact row under the sample panel | Calmer first screen |
| 3 | Toasts overlap the Quick-tips card and the bottom bar (seen in screenshots). Use one toast stack in the top-right on desktop and above the bar on phones | Clarity |
| 4 | Quick-tips covers about a third of the model on phones: a one-line dismissible hint instead | Model first (D08) |
| 5 | Header on 1100–1300 px: the toolbar fold-out kicks in late; with Support and Convert added, test that nothing clips | Fit |

## 5. Quality-of-life backlog (small, high frequency)

1. Click any dimension in Details to copy it ("20 × 30 × 40 mm").
2. `Ctrl+Shift+E`: convert again to the last format and settings without the dialog (still stops on losses).
3. Show the converted file size in the converter before download (estimate from the triangle count for STL/OBJ).
4. Drag a file onto an open model and get "Replace · Add · New tab" without a modal: the choice already exists, so
   make it a drop-target split overlay.
5. Recents: show the format badge and real size ("0.0 MB" appears for tiny files), and let people pin favourites.
6. Remember the camera per file in recents (saved views exist; restore the last one automatically).
7. Status bar: click the triangle count to open the model health summary.
8. Measurement: "Copy all as CSV" next to "Download JSON".
9. Command palette: show recent commands first; typing a file extension ("stl") offers "Convert to STL".
10. Batch: allow removing a job, and "Convert all again with these settings" (review D04).
11. When a texture is missing, offer "Pick the folder" right in the toast.
12. Keyboard shortcut sheet: printable and searchable.
13. Empty states with one clear action everywhere (Materials tab, Saved views, Problems).

## 7. Accessibility

What exists: named controls, focus rings , a skip link, reduced-motion
setting, numeric transform input, and a labelled export dialog. What to do next:

- The converter list is a `radiogroup` of buttons. Use roving `tabindex` and `aria-checked` updates without
  re-rendering, so screen readers don't lose their place (it currently re-renders on choice and restores focus).
- Task chips are buttons with text; keep them that way (no icon-only chips).
- Announce load results ("Opened bracket.stl, 1 part, 20 × 30 × 40 mm") through the existing live region.
- The monochrome signal is maximum contrast in both themes (white on near-black, black on light grey).
- Phase B tests from the review (NVDA and VoiceOver task runs) still apply.

## 9. Growth and SEO

What the site already does well: canonical URLs, hreflang across five languages, a complete sitemap, structured
data and share images. What this branch adds: guide pages for closed formats, new converter pairs, verification
hooks, IndexNow and a truthful `lastmod`. Next, in order:

1. **Verify Search Console and Bing** (`docs/search-console.md`). Without this you can't see what people search for.
2. After 2–4 weeks, read Search Console → Queries. Rewrite titles and descriptions for pages with impressions but
   few clicks. Add converter pairs for the queries that show up (likely "stl to step", "obj to fbx",
   "glb to fbx": the features ranked 1–2 above unlock these).
3. Translate the 10 guide pages into es and de once a native speaker has reviewed the existing strings.
4. Distribution, no ads needed: maker communities, AlternativeTo, Product Hunt, GitHub awesome-lists, and a short
   demo video of "drop an F3D, get the steps; drop a STEP, get a 3MF in 10 seconds".
5. Keep the promise: no analytics in the page. Search Console and Bing Webmaster data is enough to steer.
